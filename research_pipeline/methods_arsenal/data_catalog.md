### Social Media — Detailed

#### TikTok Research API (Official)
- **URL:** https://developers.tiktok.com/products/research-api/
- **Access:** Academic application via DSC (EU) or university application (US)
- **Volume:** Public content + accounts; rate-limited
- **Recency:** Live, real-time
- **Research uses:** Brand hashtag virality; branded short-video engagement analysis
- ⚠️ **Restriction:** US and Europe only; separate approval needed for Douyin (China)

#### Twitter/X Academic Research API
- **URL:** https://developer.x.com/en/use-cases/do-research/academic-research
- **Access:** Application to X Developer Portal
- **Volume:** Full-archive with elevated access
- **Recency:** Live + historical
- **Research uses:** Brand sentiment; product launch discourse
- ⚠️ **FLAG: Cost barrier** — commercial tiers start ~$42K/month; academic pricing requires negotiation. Pay-per-use in closed beta Dec 2025. EU DSA pressure has not restored free access.

#### Instagram Graph API v25
- **URL:** https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2026/02/18/introducing-graph-api-v25-and-marketing-api-v25/
- **Access:** Meta Developer app; Business/Creator account needed
- **Volume:** Per-account analytics; demographic data limited to top-45 segments
- **Recency:** Live; v25.0 released Feb 2026
- **Research uses:** Brand visual content analysis; influencer engagement benchmarking
- ⚠️ Jan 2025: video_views, email_contacts, profile_views deprecated

#### Xiaohongshu AIGC Comments & Posts Dataset
- **URL:** https://github.com/coralr-1/Xiaohongshu-AIGC-Comments-and-Posts-Dataset
- **Access:** GitHub free download
- **Volume:** Posts + comments on AIGC topics; user IDs, timestamps, likes, sentiment labels
- **Recency:** ~2024 snapshot
- **Research uses:** Chinese consumer attitudes to AI-generated brand content
- ⚠️ **China PIPL:** Verify IRB clearance; collection method unclear

#### RedNote-Vibe (5-year Xiaohongshu longitudinal)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22055v1
- **Access:** arXiv paper + supplementary data
- **Volume:** 5-year longitudinal; pre-LLM era to July 2025; engagement metrics per post
- **Recency:** Through July 2025
- **Research uses:** Longitudinal brand content evolution; AI vs. authentic content discrimination

#### Weibo Open API
- **URL:** https://open.weibo.com/
- **Access:** Developer application; academic research requires approval
- **Volume:** Public posts, profiles, trending topics (rate-limited)
- **Recency:** Live; policy last reviewed 2023–2024
- **Research uses:** Chinese brand social listening; sentiment on domestic vs. foreign brands
- ⚠️ **China PIPL + ToS:** Bulk scraping violates ToS; academic approval process slow

### E-commerce — Detailed

#### Amazon Reviews 2023
- **URL:** https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/
- **Hugging Face:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/McAuley-Lab/Amazon-Reviews-2023
- **Volume:** ~571M reviews; 33 product categories; May 1996 – Sep 2023
- **Research uses:** Brand equity from review sentiment; cross-category loyalty/switching

#### JD.com Reviews Dataset
- **URL:** http://yongfeng.me/dataset/
- **Volume:** ~60M reviews; ~2M users; 100K+ products; Jan 2011–Mar 2014
- **Research uses:** Chinese e-commerce brand reputation; price sensitivity (structured pos/neg/overall sub-reviews)
- ⚠️ Data dated (pre-2015); useful as longitudinal baseline

#### Kaggle Social Media & Consumer Behavior (2025)
- **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jocelyndumlao/social-media-and-consumer-behavior-2025
- **Published:** November 2025
- **Research uses:** Algorithm influence on purchase intent; social commerce adoption

#### Kaggle Marketing Campaign Performance
- **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/manishabhatt22/marketing-campaign-performance-dataset
- **Research uses:** Cross-channel ROI benchmarking; brand awareness modeling

### Academic — Detailed

#### ICPSR Survey of Consumer Attitudes & Behavior
- **URL:** https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/ICPSR/series/00054/variables
- **Volume:** Monthly panel waves; continuous since 1940s; 2025 waves available
- **Research uses:** Consumer sentiment index as macro control variable; longitudinal purchase intention tracking

#### Harvard Dataverse IRI Marketing Data
- **URL:** https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/YQQSLM
- **Volume:** 5–6 years; 30 CPG categories; 49 store markets + 2 consumer panel markets
- **Research uses:** Brand-level price elasticity; competitive positioning for CPG brands

#### WRDS (Wharton Research Data Services)
- **URL:** https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/
- **Access:** Institutional subscription; DMEF dataset (~100K customers) available
- **Research uses:** Direct marketing response modeling; linking brand value with consumer outcomes
