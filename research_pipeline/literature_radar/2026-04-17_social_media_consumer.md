### How Does Brand-Generated Content Engage Consumers? A Study on Fashion Brands' Instagram Data

- **Authors:** Xiaoru Gao, S. Chan Choi, Sunghoon Kim, Kihyun Kim, Madhavi Chakrabarty
- **Journal:** Journal of Interactive Marketing | **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** [10.1177/10949968251335616](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10949968251335616)
- **Platform studied:** Instagram
- **Abstract summary:** Examines how design features of brand-generated posts affect consumer engagement on Instagram fashion brand accounts. The authors use machine learning to extract visual and textual design features from posts, then apply Poisson regression to measure their differential effects on engagement (likes). Addresses the gap in understanding which specific design elements maximize engagement in a competitive social media landscape.
- **Methodology:** Machine learning (automated feature extraction from images/text) + Poisson regression
- **Data type:** Secondary / scraped (Instagram posts from major fashion brands)
- **Sample size:** Not disclosed in accessible abstract; large-scale post-level dataset
- **Key findings:** Inserting URLs in posts significantly reduces engagement; increasing the number of images in a single post is generally effective; design-level features have measurable, differential effects on consumer interactions.
- **Research gap signaled:** Extension to other platforms (TikTok, Xiaohongshu); engagement metrics beyond likes (saves, shares, story views); longitudinal dynamics of content effectiveness.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★★  
  > 🚩 **Metric flag:** Moves beyond simple like/comment counts — uses ML-extracted design features as independent variables, treating post design as a multidimensional construct. High-priority paper.

### The Effect of Visual Perspective on Consumer Viewing and Purchase Intention in Live Streaming

- **Authors:** Tang, Z. Hao, et al.
- **Journal:** Journal of Consumer Behaviour | **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** [10.1002/cb.70069](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cb.70069)
- **Volume/Issue/Pages:** Vol. 25, No. 1, pp. 362–377
- **Platform studied:** General (live streaming e-commerce)
- **Abstract summary:** Grounded in sensory marketing theory, this three-study paper examines how first-person vs. third-person visual perspectives in live streams affect consumers' continued viewing and purchase intentions. Virtual tactility and flow experience are proposed and tested as mediators, with product type (functional vs. hedonic) as a moderator.
- **Methodology:** Three-study design — questionnaire survey (Study 1) + controlled experiments (Studies 2 & 3)
- **Data type:** Survey / experiment
- **Sample size:** Three independent samples (sizes not accessible)
- **Key findings:** Third-person perspective is generally more effective in stimulating viewing and purchase intentions; functional products perform better under first-person perspective while hedonic products perform better under third-person perspective; virtual tactility and flow experience are validated mediators.
- **Research gap signaled:** Real-world field studies; AR/VR streaming contexts; cross-cultural comparison of perspective preferences.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★  
  > 🚩 **Metric flag:** Introduces *virtual tactility* as a novel perceptual construct for measuring immersive engagement in live commerce, extending beyond behavioral metrics.

### The Effect of E-Commerce Live Streamer Partner Type on Consumers' Approach Intention: An Empirical Study Based on Live Streaming Studios

- **Authors:** Xiong et al.
- **Journal:** Journal of Consumer Behaviour | **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** [10.1002/cb.70084](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cb.70084)
- **Platform studied:** General (live streaming e-commerce)
- **Abstract summary:** Investigates how the combination of streamer partner types — human–human, human–virtual, or virtual–virtual — influences consumers' approach intention in e-commerce live streaming. Based on empirical data from actual live streaming studio settings, the study addresses the growing use of virtual (AI-generated) streamers in commercial broadcasting.
- **Methodology:** Empirical study (field/experimental in live streaming studio settings)
- **Data type:** Not confirmed; field/experimental
- **Sample size:** Not confirmed
- **Key findings:** Different streamer-partner combinations produce distinct effects on consumer approach intention; virtual streamers are not equivalent substitutes for human streamers in terms of psychological approach responses.
- **Research gap signaled:** Longitudinal adoption of AI streamers; consumer trust calibration toward virtual vs. human streamers; effects across product categories.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★

### Hooked on Livestreaming: What Drives Customer Repurchase Intention in E-Commerce?

- **Authors:** Linh-Gia Ngoc Phan, Dang Quan Tri, Son-Hoang Dang, Luan-Thanh Nguyen
- **Journal:** Journal of Creative Communications (SAGE) | **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** [10.1177/09732586241311001](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/09732586241311001)
- **Platform studied:** General (e-commerce livestreaming)
- **Abstract summary:** Applies customer inspiration theory (CIT) and social cognitive theory (SCT) to explain psychological mechanisms driving repeat purchase behavior in livestreaming contexts. The model tests technical environment factors (interactivity), personal factors (perceived value, self-efficacy), and customer experience (hedonic and utilitarian outcome expectations) as antecedents.
- **Methodology:** PLS-SEM (Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling)
- **Data type:** Survey
- **Sample size:** N = 158
- **Key findings:** Perceived value and both hedonic and utilitarian outcome expectations positively shape the "inspired-by" state; interactivity contributes to inspiration; "inspired-to" state directly drives repurchase behavioral intention.
- **Research gap signaled:** Larger cross-cultural samples; platform-specific moderators (TikTok Shop vs. Taobao Live); longitudinal repurchase tracking.
- **Relevance:** ★★★

### Leveraging Employees as Social Media Advocates: The Impacts of Social Media Communication Strategies on Employees' Social Media Engagement

- **Authors:** Yeunjae Lee, Xiao Ma, Tanzid Basunia, Ruining Hu
- **Journal:** International Journal of Business Communication (SAGE) | **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** [10.1177/23294884251409667](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/23294884251409667)
- **Platform studied:** General (organizational social media)
- **Abstract summary:** Examines how internal social media communication strategies (organization-oriented vs. employee-oriented) influence employees' engagement behaviors on social media as brand advocates. Tests organizational identification and positive emotions as mediating mechanisms.
- **Methodology:** Survey / experimental (not confirmed from accessible metadata)
- **Data type:** Survey
- **Sample size:** Not confirmed
- **Key findings:** Organization-oriented strategies enhanced employees' organizational identification, motivating in-role and extra-role social media behaviors; employee-oriented strategies fostered engagement primarily through positive emotional activation.
- **Research gap signaled:** Platform-specific effects (LinkedIn vs. Instagram); interaction with external brand reputation; long-term advocacy sustainability.
- **Relevance:** ★★★

### Do Social Media Marketing Activities Help Create Masstige Brand Value of Fashion Brands Among Upper-Middle-Class Working Women?

- **Authors:** Mallika Srivastava, Mudita Sinha
- **Journal:** Vision: The Journal of Business Perspective (SAGE) | **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** [10.1177/22779752251414829](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/22779752251414829)
- **Platform studied:** General social media (fashion brands)
- **Abstract summary:** Investigates whether social media marketing activities (SMMA) — including entertainment, interaction, trendiness, advertisements, and customization — predict masstige brand value and brand loyalty among upper-middle-class working women. Provides segment-specific insights for fashion brand strategy.
- **Methodology:** Survey-based structural equation modeling (SEM)
- **Data type:** Survey
- **Sample size:** Not confirmed
- **Key findings:** SMMA dimensions (entertainment, interaction, trendiness, advertisements, customization) positively impact masstige brand value; masstige brand value mediates the relationship between SMMA and brand loyalty.
- **Research gap signaled:** Cross-cultural replication; luxury vs. masstige brand distinctions; platform-specific SMMA effects.
- **Relevance:** ★★★

### eWOM and Content Marketing Strategies: Maximizing Brand Engagement Among Social Media Users in Spain

- **Authors:** Not accessible (paywall)
- **Journal:** ScienceDirect (ISSN 2444-8834) | **Year:** Published August 28, 2025
- **DOI:** [S2444883425000257](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2444883425000257)
- **Platform studied:** Instagram + TikTok
- **Abstract summary:** Analyzes how brands' content marketing strategies across Instagram and TikTok influence eWOM, operationalized as the volume of likes and comments. Tests brand consistency, post position, message format, celebrity endorsement, content attractiveness, and interactivity as predictors.
- **Methodology:** Content analysis + Negative binomial regression
- **Data type:** Secondary / scraped (brand posts)
- **Sample size:** N = 500 posts (250 Instagram + 250 TikTok from top-interaction brands in Spain)
- **Key findings:** Platform-specific effects: message format drives eWOM on TikTok; content attractiveness is more influential on Instagram. Celebrity endorsement increases likes on both platforms and comments on TikTok.
- **Research gap signaled:** Longitudinal analysis; non-Western markets; platform algorithm interaction with content strategy.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★ (outside date window; included for methodology quality)
