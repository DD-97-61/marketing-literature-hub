### Influence of Audiovisual Features of Short Video Advertising on Consumer Engagement Behaviors: Evidence from TikTok

- **Authors:** Zhipeng Zhang, Keda Qiu, Yan Ye
- **Journal:** Journal of Business Research | **Year:** 2025 (Vol. 201)
- **Platform studied:** TikTok
- **Abstract summary:** This study investigates how sensory information embedded in short video advertisements affects consumer engagement behaviors. Using machine learning and deep learning, the authors extract audiovisual features — cadence, colorfulness, colloquial expression, and visual prominence — from TikTok ads, and integrate them with multimodal theory and the Elaboration Likelihood Model to explain engagement variation.
- **Methodology:** Machine learning + deep learning feature extraction; multimodal theoretical framework; regression analysis
- **Data type:** Secondary / scraped (TikTok advertisement videos)
- **Sample size:** Not specified in abstract; large-scale scraped dataset
- **Key findings:** Cadence, colorfulness, colloquial expression, and visual prominence are each significantly correlated with consumer engagement behaviors (likes, comments, shares) on TikTok.
- **Research gap signaled:** Future work could extend to cross-platform comparisons (e.g., Instagram Reels vs. TikTok) and examine interaction effects among audiovisual features.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★★

### Community and Consumer Dynamics in NFTs: Understanding Digital Asset Value Through Social Engagement

- **Authors:** Kristina Brahmstaedt
- **Journal:** Journal of Consumer Behaviour | **Year:** 2025 (Vol. 24, Issue 4, pp. 1630–1655)
- **Platform studied:** Blockchain / NFT communities (Discord)
- **Abstract summary:** This qualitative study examines how community dynamics and social engagement processes drive consumer participation in NFT markets and influence digital asset valuation. Drawing on in-depth interviews with 21 NFT consumers and netnographic analysis of over 109,517 words of Discord interactions, the paper develops a framework tracing the evolution from initial purchase to sustained or discontinued engagement.
- **Methodology:** Qualitative — in-depth interviews (n = 21) + netnographic analysis of Discord community interactions
- **Data type:** Interviews + scraped/observed community text (109,517 words)
- **Sample size:** 21 interview participants; 109,517 words of Discord data
- **Key findings:** While profit expectations drive initial NFT purchases, strong community bonds and social identity formation are critical for maintaining engagement; active community participation creates a self-reinforcing cycle where engagement directly influences NFT valuation. Disillusionment arises from unfulfilled profit expectations and perceived community abandonment by project leaders.
- **Research gap signaled:** Future research should examine how platform design features shape community formation; longitudinal studies of NFT community lifecycles needed.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★★  
  🚩 **FLAG: Digital assets / NFT community / blockchain social**

### eWOM and Content Marketing Strategies: Maximizing Brand Engagement among Social Media Users in Spain

- **Authors:** Carlos Osorio-Andrade, Pericles Asher Rospigliosi, Ana Jiménez Zarco
- **Journal:** European Research on Management and Business Economics | **Year:** 2025 (DOI: 10.1016/j.iedeen.2025.100293)
- **Platform studied:** Instagram, TikTok
- **Abstract summary:** This study analyzes how brands' content marketing strategies on Instagram and TikTok drive eWOM — operationalized as volume of likes and comments — among social media users in Spain. It tests the role of brand consistency, post position, message format, celebrity endorsement, content attractiveness, and interactivity using a large sample of brand posts.
- **Methodology:** Content analysis of brand posts; negative binomial regression; control variables (posting day, emoji use, post length)
- **Data type:** Secondary / scraped (500 posts: 250 Instagram + 250 TikTok from top-interaction consumer brands)
- **Sample size:** 500 posts
- **Key findings:** Platform-specific patterns emerge: message format drives eWOM on TikTok, while content attractiveness is more influential on Instagram. Celebrity endorsement is the only variable showing consistent effects across both platforms (increases likes on both; increases comments on TikTok).
- **Research gap signaled:** Cross-cultural replication; longitudinal tracking; investigation of algorithm-driven visibility as moderator.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★☆

- **Platform studied:** General (multi-platform)
- **Abstract summary:** This conceptual paper examines the value chains underpinning influencer marketing within the growing creator economy, mapping interactions among four key actors: firms, influencers, followers, and digital platforms. - **Methodology:** Conceptual framework development; value chain analysis; integrative theoretical review
- **Data type:** Conceptual
- **Sample size:** N/A

- **Research gap signaled:** Empirical validation of the value chain framework; creator token and monetization mechanism research; platform-specific algorithm effects on creator income distribution.

### Sales through Note-Sharing: Influences on the Shopping Behavior of "Xiaohongshu" Users

- **Authors:** Guanghong Xie, Xiyuan Wang
- **Journal:** Frontiers in Psychology | **Year:** 2025 (Vol. 15, Article 1334637; published May 2025; recently indexed in PMC)
- **Platform studied:** Xiaohongshu (小红书 / RedNote)
- **Abstract summary:** This study investigates the factors influencing online purchase intention in Xiaohongshu's distinctive "Sales through Note-Sharing" model, extending the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) to include social identity, social comparison, knowledge-sharing willingness, and interface design as key variables.
- **Methodology:** Survey; confirmatory factor analysis (CFA); structural equation modeling (SEM) via SPSS and AMOS
- **Data type:** Survey
- **Sample size:** n = 287 Xiaohongshu users
- **Key findings:** Interface design has a greater impact on purchase intention than knowledge-sharing willingness; social identity significantly influences social comparison, which in turn affects behavioral intention and interface design perception; the unique "note-sharing + purchase" model is mediated by human-computer interaction factors.
- **Research gap signaled:** Future research should examine how Xiaohongshu's recommendation algorithm moderates the note-sharing-to-purchase path; cross-platform comparison with Weibo or Douyin warranted.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★☆  
  🚩 **FLAG: Chinese platform (Xiaohongshu)**

### Influence of Livestream Selling on Purchase Intention for Fashion Products on TikTok

- **Authors:** Dao Cam Thuy, Nguyen Ngoc Quang
- **Journal:** SAGE Open | **Year:** 2025 (Vol. 15, Issue 3; DOI: 10.1177/21582440251375733)
- **Platform studied:** TikTok
- **Abstract summary:** This study evaluates how livestream sales elements — engaging content and streamer characteristics — affect customer attitude, trust, and purchase intention for fashion products on TikTok, and tests promotional programs as a moderator in the live shopping process.
- **Methodology:** Structural equation modeling (SEM); exploratory factor analysis (EFA); confirmatory factor analysis (CFA); moderation analysis
- **Data type:** Survey
- **Sample size:** n = 324 TikTok livestream fashion shoppers
- **Key findings:** Attractive content and streamers contribute significantly to livestream sales effectiveness via attitude and trust. Promotional programs influence purchase intention directly but negatively moderate the attitude-to-purchase relationship, suggesting over-promotion may undermine attitudinal pathways.
- **Research gap signaled:** Future research should explore long-term relationship effects between streamer and consumer; AI-generated streamers vs. human streamers comparison needed.
- **Relevance:** ★★★☆☆

### The Impact of Social Media Information Dissemination on Consumer Brand Perception: An Empirical Study Based on Weibo and Xiaohongshu Data

- **Authors:** Pu, Y., Ma, R., Li, J., Wei, Y., Pan, X., Zhang, X.
- **Journal:** Journal of Organizational and End User Computing (JOEUC) | **Year:** 2025 (Vol. 37, Issue 1, pp. 1–33)
- **Platform studied:** Weibo, Xiaohongshu
- **Abstract summary:** This paper proposes a Multi-Dimensional Perception Modeling (MPM) framework that incorporates content semantics, social engagement signals, and platform-level features to model consumer brand perception on Chinese social media. BERT-based encoders and attention mechanisms are used to capture cross-platform variation in how brand-related information dissemination shapes perception.
- **Methodology:** NLP / deep learning (BERT-based encoders + attention mechanisms); Multi-Dimensional Perception Modeling (MPM) framework
- **Data type:** Secondary / scraped (Weibo and Xiaohongshu social media data)
- **Sample size:** Not specified; large-scale scraped dataset
- **Key findings:** Content semantics, social engagement signals (likes, reposts, comments), and platform-level affordances all independently influence consumer brand perception; cross-platform differences between Weibo and Xiaohongshu are significant and require platform-specific modeling.
- **Research gap signaled:** Extension to real-time monitoring; integration of video/image modalities beyond text; cross-brand longitudinal analysis.
- **Relevance:** ★★★★★  
  🚩 **FLAG: Chinese platforms (Weibo + Xiaohongshu); large-scale secondary data**
