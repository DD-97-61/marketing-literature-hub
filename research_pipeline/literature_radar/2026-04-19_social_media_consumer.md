### Influence of Audiovisual Features of Short Video Advertising on Consumer Engagement Behaviors: Evidence from TikTok
- **Authors:** Zhang, Qiu, and Ye
- **Journal:** Journal of Business Research | **Year:** 2025 (Vol. 201)
- **Platform studied:** TikTok
- **Abstract summary:** Analyzes TikTok short video ad audiovisual features and their effects on consumer engagement using machine and deep learning feature extraction. Integrates multimodal theory and the elaboration likelihood model. Identifies key content signals predictive of engagement across ad categories.
- **Methodology:** Machine/deep learning feature extraction + regression analysis on scraped TikTok data
- **Data type:** Secondary/scraped (TikTok advertisements)
- **Sample size:** Not reported in search results
- **Key findings:** Four audiovisual features—colloquial expression, cadence, colorfulness, and visual prominence—significantly drive consumer engagement. Product type and release timing moderate these effects.
- **Research gap signaled:** Cross-platform generalizability; long-form vs. short-form video dynamics; cultural variation in aesthetic preferences
- **Relevance:** ★★★★★

### Influencer Marketing Effectiveness: A Meta-Analytic Review
- **Authors:** Not fully reported in search results
- **Journal:** Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science (JAMS) | **Year:** 2025 (Vol. 53, No. 1)
- **Platform studied:** General (social media)
- **Abstract summary:** Comprehensive meta-analysis of 251 papers and 1,531 effect sizes on influencer marketing effectiveness. Maps antecedents, mediators, and moderators for both non-transactional outcomes (attitude, behavioral engagement, purchase intention) and transactional outcomes (purchase behavior, sales).
- **Methodology:** Meta-analysis
- **Data type:** Secondary (meta-analytic)
- **Sample size:** 251 papers, 1,531 effect sizes
- **Key findings:** Post, follower, and influencer characteristics each independently predict influencer marketing outcomes. Effects on transactional outcomes are weaker and more variable than on attitudinal/intentional outcomes.
- **Research gap signaled:** Actual purchase vs. self-reported intention gap; platform algorithm moderation of influencer reach; non-Western market applicability
- **Relevance:** ★★★★★

- **Authors:** Bazi, Filieri, Gorton
- **Journal:** Internet Research | **Year:** 2026
- **Platform studied:** Social media (luxury brand context)
- **Abstract summary:** Investigates negative downstream consequences of customer engagement with luxury brands on social media, including reduced self-esteem, heightened materialism, technology addiction, and impulsive buying. Identifies gender as a significant moderator of outcomes.
- **Methodology:** Survey (quantitative, SEM)
- **Data type:** Survey
- **Sample size:** Not reported in search results

- **Relevance:** ★★★★☆

### Artificial Intelligence-Powered Digital Streamers in Online Retail: Empirical Insights and Design Strategies from Experiments
- **Authors:** Yahui Liu, Lei Wang, Shuai Yang, Yanwen Wang
- **Journal:** Information Systems Research (ISR) | **Year:** 2025
- **Platform studied:** Tmall (live streaming commerce)
- **Abstract summary:** Uses quasi-experimental and field-experimental methods to assess AI-powered digital streamers' economic impact on product sales. Compares human streamers, AI digital streamers, and no-streaming conditions across a fashion retailer on Tmall. Tests how form realism and behavioral realism (interactivity, Q&A) enhance AI streamer effectiveness.
- **Methodology:** Quasi-experimental (difference-in-differences) + controlled field experiments
- **Data type:** Secondary (platform sales data) + experimental
- **Sample size:** 328 products (72 digital streamer, 74 human streamer, 182 no-streaming condition)
- **Key findings:** Human streamers strongly boost sales; AI digital streamers show only modest uplift vs. no streaming. Adding real-time Q&A raises AI streamer sales volume by 25% and revenue by 86%; a lottery interaction feature raises sales 17% and revenue 70%.
- **Research gap signaled:** Consumer trust in AI streamers over extended exposure; category-level moderation (high-involvement vs. low-involvement products); optimal human–AI hybrid streamer configurations
- **Relevance:** ★★★★★
