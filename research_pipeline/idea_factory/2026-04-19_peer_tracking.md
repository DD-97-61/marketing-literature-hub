- **Paper:** "The effect of brand alliances across dependent variables and research designs: A meta-analysis"
- **Venue:** Journal of Business Research, Vol. 191, 2025
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2025.115003
- **Topic:** Meta-analytic synthesis of co-branding/brand alliance effects. Mean effect size = 0.244. Finds that fictional brands show larger effects on alliance attitudes; real brands drive stronger focal brand attitude effects. - **Overlap with our interests:** **HIGH** — comprehensive meta-analysis covering the full co-branding effect literature (subsuming co-branding, ingredient branding, advertising alliances). This effectively "closes the book" on simple main-effect questions about whether co-branding works.
